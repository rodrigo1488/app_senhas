CompuFlow is the Computicket queue app. Use it on a tablet, kiosk, operator phone or waiting-room TV.

With a sector code, the same app becomes:

• Customer kiosk — issue regular or priority tickets
• Operator panel — call the next ticket and manage service
• Feedback screen — satisfaction survey after service
• TV display — called tickets and ads, landscape or portrait

Connect to your company server. The default address is https://apisenhas.computicket.space. You can also scan the local network or enter another address.

The app does not create a Google account. The session stays on the device and service data is sent to your company server.
